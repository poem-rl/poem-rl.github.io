More responses for the larger reward model, in the format of the page. The image examples from the diffusion models follow.

Larger reward model

πtar is RL on the 8B reward model, and PoEM composes the experts trained with RL on its 3B and 4B siblings. On these prompts PoEM writes the way πtar does; over 53 short prompts, blind reviewers found PoEM's response closest to πtar's on 27, the top expert's on 16 and the reference model's on 10. Where every response restates the prompt first, rows start at the first item, and on the wildebeest prompt at the fourth, which PoEM and πtar write word for word.

Image diffusion models

Composing the experts trained on the other rewards reproduces the change the target model makes, such as more saturated colors, finer texture, and a centered subject on a blurred background. The held-out expert's changes lie largely within what the other experts can express.
